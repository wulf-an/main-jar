

## IELTS Speaking Mock Test (30 minutes)

Copy and paste the prompt below into your AI assistant:

📝 Act as an official IELTS Speaking examiner. Conduct a complete IELTS Speaking test with me. Ask one question at a time. After I answer, continue naturally. At the end, estimate my IELTS band score and provide detailed feedback on my Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy, and Pronunciation (if using voice mode). Also suggest how I can improve to achieve Band 7 or above.
