# TYPES OF COMPANIES -

## 1. Product Based Company

A product-based IT company is a business that develops and sells software or hardware products. Instead of offering services like consulting or IT support, these companies focus on creating products that they sell to customers. These products can include software applications, mobile apps, operating systems, and hardware devices.
Examples of product-based IT companies include:
➢ Microsoft: Known for its Windows operating system, Office productivity suite, and
various other software products.
➢ Apple: Famous for its iPhone, iPad, Mac computers, and software like iOS and
macOS.
➢ Adobe: Known for its creative software products like Photoshop, Illustrator, and
Acrobat.
➢ Google: Offers a range of products including the Android operating system, Google
Search, Gmail, and Google Docs.
➢ Oracle: Provides database software and technology, cloud engineered systems, and
enterprise software products.
These companies invest heavily in research and development to create innovative products
that meet the needs of their customers and stay competitive in the market.


## Service Based Company

A service-based IT company is a business that provides various IT-related services to its clients. Instead of selling physical products, these companies offer intangible services that help clients with their technology needs. These services can include software development, IT consulting, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and digital marketing, among others.
Examples of service-based IT companies include:
➢ Infosys: A global leader in consulting, technology, outsourcing,and next-generation services.
➢ Accenture: A multinational professional services company that provides services in strategy, consulting, digital, technology, and operations.
➢ Wipro: An Indian multinational corporation that provides information technology, consulting, and business process services.
➢ Cognizant: An American multinational corporation that provides IT services, including digital, technology, consulting, and operations services.
➢ IBM: A multinational technology company that offers a range of IT services, including cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity.
These companies play a crucial role in helping other businesses adopt and utilise technology to improve their operations, enhance efficiency, and achieve their business goals.
