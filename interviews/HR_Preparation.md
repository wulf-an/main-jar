## HR Questions to Prepare

What are your long-term career goals: Discuss your aspirations for growth and advancement in your field. Explain how this role fits into your long-term career plan and how you plan to achieve your goals.
How do you prioritise your work: Explain your method for prioritising tasks based on deadlines, importance, and impact. Emphasise your ability to manage multiple projects efficiently.
What is your greatest professional achievement: Share a specific accomplishment that demonstrates your skills, determination, and contribution to your organisation. Explain why it is meaningful to you and how it has helped you grow professionally
How do you handle constructive criticism: Demonstrate your ability to accept feedback positively and use it to improve. Describe a specific example of when you received constructive criticism and how you implemented it.

Mirror Talk: Speak in front of a mirror for 5 minutes daily about any topic.
