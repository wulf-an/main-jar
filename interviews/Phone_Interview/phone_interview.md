HR might make a call before scheduling an interview for a few reasons:
Screening: They may want to do a quick check to see if you meet the basic requirements for the job, such as availability, salary expectations, or relevant experience.
Clarification: They might want to clarify information from your resume or cover letter, such as gaps in employment or details about your previous roles.
Interest: They could be calling to gauge your interest in the position and to see if you're still available and interested in moving forward with the interview process.
Communication Skills: A phone call can also help them assess your communication skills, which are important for many roles.
Overall, the call is a way for HR to gather more information about you and determine if you're a good fit for the position before scheduling a formal interview.
