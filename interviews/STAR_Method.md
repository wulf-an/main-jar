# STAR Method

The STAR method is a technique used to answer behavioural interview questions. It stands for Situation, Task, Action, and Result. Here's a breakdown of each part:
Situation: Describe the specific situation or context you were in. Provide enough detail so the interviewer understands the background of your example.
Task: Explain the task or goal you were working towards in that situation. What were you trying to achieve?
Action: Describe the actions you took to address the situation or complete the task. Focus on what you did, not what your team or others did. Explain your thought process and any steps you took to resolve the situation.
Result: Finally, explain the results of your actions. What happened as a result of your efforts? Try to quantify the results if possible (e.g., "increased sales by 20%," "reduced customer complaints by 50%").
Using the STAR method helps you structure your response in a clear and concise manner, making it easier for the interviewer to understand your example and assess your skills and abilities.
